What actually matters in a fight

Fans — your health bar. First side to lose 20% of their fans loses the battle.
Sing + Dance — your raw power, before any bonuses are applied.
Normal Attack — extra damage every second of the fight.
Skill Damage — boosts a big burst hit that lands every 11 seconds.
Reductions — cut the damage coming back at you.
Genre bonus — 3 girls of one genre gives +15% stats, 4 gives +20%, 5 gives +25%.
